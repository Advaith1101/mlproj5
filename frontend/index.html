Validated Joins

  • ✓ MGNREGA to District: Filtered to FY 2024-25 and aggregated N:1 computing annual persondays and expenditure.

Enforced Grain Prohibitions

  • ✕ Direct State-to-District 1:N Join: Prohibited. Joining state-level EV tables directly into district rows causes spurious duplicate repetition.
